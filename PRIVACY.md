# Privacy Policy for IBAN Validator Extension

**Last Updated**: October 15, 2025

## Overview

The IBAN Validator browser extension is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how the extension handles information when you use it.

## Data Collection

**We DO NOT collect any personal information or data.**

### What We Don't Collect
- ❌ IBAN numbers you enter
- ❌ Personal information
- ❌ Browsing history
- ❌ Usage analytics
- ❌ Device information
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Any form of user tracking

## How the Extension Works

### Client-Side Only Operation
- All IBAN validation and generation is performed **locally** on your device
- **No data is transmitted** to external servers
- **No internet connection** is required for validation or generation
- Your IBAN data **never leaves your browser**

### Permissions
The extension requests **zero permissions** from your browser, ensuring:
- No access to your browsing data
- No access to other websites
- No access to your files or system
- Complete isolation from other browser activities

## Data Storage

### Local Storage
- **No data is stored** permanently by the extension
- IBAN inputs and generated samples are only held temporarily in memory during use
- Data is automatically cleared when you close the extension popup
- No validation history or cache is maintained

## Third-Party Services

### External Dependencies
- **None**: The extension uses no external APIs or services
- **No tracking pixels** or analytics tools
- **No advertisements** or third-party content
- Complete standalone operation

## Future Updates

### Planned Features
As this extension is a work in progress, future versions may include:
- Optional validation history (stored locally only)
- User preferences (stored locally only)
- Dark mode settings (stored locally only)

**Promise**: Any future features will maintain the same privacy-first approach:
- Data will remain local to your device
- No external data transmission
- User control over all data storage
- Transparent privacy practices

### Privacy Policy Updates
If future versions require any changes to this privacy policy:
- Users will be notified before updates
- Opt-in consent will be required for any data handling
- This policy will be updated with clear change logs

## Technical Implementation

### Security Measures
