# Privacy Policy — IBAN Validator

**Last Updated**: September 8, 2026

## Summary

The IBAN Validator extension collects no data. All validation and generation happens locally in your browser. Nothing is ever transmitted anywhere.

## Data Collection

We collect no data of any kind — no IBANs you enter, no usage analytics, no device information, no browsing history, no location data.

## How It Works

All processing is done entirely on your device. The extension requires no internet connection and makes no network requests. Data entered into the extension exists only in memory and is cleared when you close the popup.

## Permissions

The extension requests `activeTab` and `scripting` permissions exclusively to support the Scan Page feature. These are only activated when you explicitly click the Scan Page button. No page content is stored, transmitted, or retained in any form after the scan completes.

## Third-Party Services

None. The extension uses no external APIs, analytics tools, tracking pixels, or advertisements.

## Future Updates

Any future features that involve local storage (such as validation history or preferences) will store data on your device only, with full user control. This policy will be updated transparently if anything changes.

## Contact

For questions or concerns, open an issue at [github.com/berno9/IBAN-Validator](https://github.com/berno9/IBAN-Validator).
